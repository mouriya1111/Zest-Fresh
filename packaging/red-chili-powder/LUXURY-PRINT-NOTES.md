# ZestFresh luxury flexible-pouch redesign

This version is artwork for a laminated stand-up zipper pouch, not a carton or box.

## Format

- Artboard: 136 x 206 mm
- Trim: 130 x 200 mm
- Bleed: 3 mm on every edge
- Conservative safe area: 8 mm inside trim
- Separate front and back artwork
- CMYK print PDFs and editable Illustrator-compatible SVG files
- Front food image: CMYK, above 300 effective DPI at placed size

The 130 x 200 mm trim is a standard working size for a 100 g pouch. The pouch converter's approved seal, zipper, tear-notch, corner and gusset dieline must replace the non-printing planning guides before press.

## Gold foil

All intended gold-foil elements are isolated in the `GOLD_FOIL` layer of the front SVG. The PDF shows a warm CMYK simulation. For genuine metallic foil, the printer should create a separate 100% spot plate from this layer and confirm minimum foil line widths, trapping and registration tolerance.

## Mandatory legal completion

Replace and verify every bracketed field before printing:

- GS1-issued GTIN barcode
- FSSAI license number
- Manufacturer/packer legal name and complete address
- Batch number, MRP, packed-on date and best-before declaration
- Customer-care phone and email
- Laboratory-verified nutrition values
- Geographic-origin story, claims and label declarations with the responsible food-label compliance professional

The QR code points to `https://zestfresh.in`.

## Editable source

The SVG files retain live text for correction and can be opened directly in Adobe Illustrator. Create a duplicate and convert text to outlines after all legal copy is approved. The brand mark, Guntur field artwork, typographic ornaments and statutory symbols are vector.

For 200 g, 500 g and 1 kg sizes, reflow the artwork into each converter-provided dieline. Do not stretch the 100 g panel uniformly.
